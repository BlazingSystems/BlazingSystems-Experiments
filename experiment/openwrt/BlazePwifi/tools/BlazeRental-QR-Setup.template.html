Fresh-phone flow
  1. Factory reset or use a new/unprovisioned Android phone.
  2. At the Android welcome screen, trigger enterprise QR provisioning (commonly tap the welcome area six times; OEM behavior varies).
  3. Connect to internet if the QR does not include Wi-Fi.
  4. Scan this QR. Android downloads BlazeRental.apk, verifies its checksum, and provisions it as Device Owner.


A hardware/recovery factory wipe can remove any third-party DPC. Re-scan a fresh Device Provisioning QR after such a wipe.
